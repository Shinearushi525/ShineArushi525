## 👩‍💻 About Me

> *"Data has the power to protect people — if models are built with care, rigor, and ethics."*

I'm an **AI & Data Science** undergraduate passionate about building ML and NLP systems that solve **high-impact, real-world problems** — detecting financial fraud, understanding mental health from text, and predicting customer behavior before it happens.

My interests lie at the intersection of classical machine learning, deep learning, and modern generative AI. I focus on understanding how models behave in practice — how they fail, reason, and improve with feedback.

I prefer building systems from first principles before scaling them. This ensures that design choices are explainable, testable, and production-ready.

I believe in clean, reproducible pipelines and explainable models. A well-understood baseline beats an overfit black box every time.
